LineBound Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

LineBound is a puzzle game made by Kenneth Neads ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who can see it, and how to stop it or delete it. The short version: the game sends anonymous play stats so I can balance the levels, and if you choose a nickname, it appears on a public leaderboard. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps, and nothing is sold.

What the game collects

DataWhat it isWhy
Anonymous player id A random id created the first time the game goes online. It isn't tied to your name, email, Google account or phone number. To keep your stats together and let only you change them.
Play stats Per level: moves, time played, restarts, undos, deaths, hints used, replays, whether and how you solved it. Plus the game version and platform (Android). To find levels that are too hard, too easy or not fun, and fix them.
Level ratings Your answers after solving a level: difficulty (1-5), whether it fit its place in the chapter, and whether it was fun. Same as above.
Nickname (optional) A name you choose (2-16 letters, numbers, spaces, _ or -). To show you on the leaderboard and tell testers apart.
Leaderboard totals Stars, levels solved, total time, total hints and total restarts (best runs and first tries). Only sent if you chose a nickname. To rank players on the leaderboard.
Reports If you report a nickname as offensive: which name you reported, and when. To moderate the leaderboard.

The game does not collect your real name, email, contacts, photos, location, advertising id or any other device identifier. It doesn't use ads or third-party analytics.

Like any internet service, the hosting provider (below) may briefly record your IP address in server logs for security and to keep the service running. I don't use it to identify you.

Who can see it

  • Everyone who plays can see your nickname and leaderboard totals, if you chose a nickname. Nothing else about you is shown to other players.
  • Me, the developer, can see all of the data above, to improve the game and moderate names.
  • Supabase (supabase.com) hosts the database, in the East US (North Virginia) region. It processes the data only on my behalf. It's protected by HTTPS, and database rules only let each player read or change their own records.

I don't sell or rent your data, and I don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. I would only disclose it if the law required me to.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing: on the title screen, tap Privacy and turn Share play stats off. Nothing more is sent from your phone, and the leaderboard is turned off for you.
  • Delete your data: in the same Privacy screen, tap Delete my online data. This deletes your ratings, play stats, nickname, leaderboard entry and reports right away. Your progress on the phone stays.
  • By email: if you've uninstalled the game or the button doesn't work, email treyneads@outlook.com with your player id (shown in the Privacy screen) or your nickname, and I'll delete your data within 30 days.
  • No nickname: picking a nickname is optional. Without one you're never on the leaderboard.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes everything stored on your phone, but not what was already sent. Use one of the options above for that.

How long it's kept

Until you delete it, or until the game is shut down, whichever comes first. Offensive nicknames may be hidden or removed at any time.

Children

LineBound is meant for players aged 13 and over. I don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent data, email me and I'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. The game's data is processed because of my legitimate interest in improving the game, and, for the nickname and leaderboard, because you chose to take part. To use any of these rights, email treyneads@outlook.com. I'm based in the United States.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what's collected, the game will say so.
